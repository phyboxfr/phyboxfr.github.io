Video


   The videos open in a player developed for PhYBoX. It was designed minimalistically, displaying only the necessary content to avoid distractions :



In the upper right corner is a small toolbar to add some functionality to it :

   Go back in the explorer to the folder where the videos came from.

   Open or close the playlist.

   Put the video in full screen.


Use :

Still in the spirit of BoX, the applications are designed to maximize the display surface of the document viewed and thus make working on a tablet more comfortable.

To simplify video playback, simple pressing on the screen has been configured as follows :

When you open a video, it starts playing automatically (in full screen), but the Reading List on the right voluntarily remains open, so that other videos present in the same folder and which could also help the student, initially, remain visible.

At the first press on the screen, the Playlist closes to enlarge the video which remains playing.

On the second press, the video is Paused and the Playlist reopens to allow the student to select another one. And so on.

Note :

Videos only appear in the player after being indexed in the database. Refer to Admin -> Indexing for more details.
