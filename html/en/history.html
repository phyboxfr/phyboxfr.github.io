Principle


This kind of device based on the pair Server | Routeur is not really original since it has existed for a while in the world of Education. However, each of these BoXs is often oriented towards a certain type of use, depending on the discipline and the specific needs encountered during its missions by the teacher who designed it.

The development of this application was based on the observation that the use of mobile tablet classes which are made available in our schools sometimes poses as many problems as it solves. This often limits its use to simple internet searches or the use of online applications.

One of the main problems lies in how to provide students with the necessary documents to help them in their work, without having to copy them onto each tablet. The observation is the same in the opposite direction, when we wish to recover possible documents produced by the students.

From the start, PhYBoX was therefore designed to centralize the management of documents from the teacher's computer, making it easier to share them with students and in return to automatically save their own work.

Over time, other applications have been integrated, but the essential part of PhYBoX remains structured to give students the simplest possible access to the various documents offered by the teacher and to retrieve their work.
