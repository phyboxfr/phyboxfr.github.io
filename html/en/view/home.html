Viewers


The PhYBoX features are distributed over several windows (Viewers or Readers), each dedicated to a specific type of activity. The readers are not all opened initially, they appear depending on the documents requested by the user.

You switch from one reader to another as soon as a new document is loaded, or by clicking on the icons in the status bar located at the bottom of the browser window :



   BoX Control Panel for Administrator.

   Panel that displays student connections on the Server.

   Viewer where different types of documents are sent to help students' work (websites, PDF files, HTML activities, etc.).

   Reader of the student activity sheet in pdf (if the file name begins with a capital letter).

   Word processing to write your work. The documents are saved on the computer, in a folder in the name of the students.

   Video player with playlist and search engine.

   File explorer of documents offered to students in the [doc] folder.

   List of PhYBoX applications.

   The Library offering in particular encyclopedias and dictionaries in wiki format (collaborative site).

   The Collaborative Board allows students to discuss, help each other and share ideas.

   Student identification and login panel.
