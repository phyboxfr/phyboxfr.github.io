Video


This application developed for the PhYBoX allows students to make a short video with the tablet, which will be saved directly in their file on the teacher's computer.


Shooting is done in a very simplified interface, without going through the camera's Android application. Students therefore do not have access to the camera's settings, which they therefore cannot modify :


Video resolution is limited to 1280x720px to reduce the size of videos on the teacher's computer. The server also limits video transfer to 200MB in order to preserve disk space. Beyond that, the video must be saved on the tablet.


Trick :

The Administrator can disable this application in the options panel, which also allows blocking the use of the tablet's camera as long as it is not needed for a given activity, in order to avoid distractions.

To do this, simply do not put a shortcut to the camera application on the tablet desktop, so that students can only launch it via the PhYBoX and only when the teacher has authorized it.
