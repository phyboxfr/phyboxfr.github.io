Photo


This application developed for the BoX allows students to take a photo with the tablet, apply some basic adjustments and save the image in their folder on the teacher's computer :


This device complements the tablet's shooting application with some additional features, to facilitate its use in a school setting.

In particular, in order to limit the weight of these images on the disk, they are automatically recompressed and resized to 1280px in the direction of their largest dimension.


Tip :

However, as the Administrator can deactivate this application in the options panel, this allows at the same time to block the use of the tablet's camera until it is needed in a given activity, in order to avoid distractions.

To do this, simply do not put a shortcut to the camera application on the tablet desktop, so that students can only launch it through PhYBoX and only when the teacher has authorized it.
