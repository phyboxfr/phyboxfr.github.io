Diagram


The PhYBoX integrates the application : Excalidraw

This application, well-suited to touch screens and easy to use, allows students to create for example a diagram, an organizational chart or a mind map, by simply linking the proposed models :

Tutoriel (en anglais) : Tutorials




For teaching Science, it also offers a Library of objects adapted to scientific diagramming (symbols of dipoles in Electricity and glassware in Chemistry), which can be easily completed according to your needs.

You can also add your own objects to the Library by clicking on one of these objects to bring up a menu and confirming the “Add to Library” option.

It is also possible to replace the existing Library by drawing it yourself to adapt it to your discipline or with one of the thematic Libraries offered on the Excalidraw site : Excalidraw Librairies

The new Library will appear the next time you launch the application, after dropping it into the following folder :

[phybox]  ->  [web]  ->  [app]  ->  [production]  ->  [diagram]  ->  [excalidraw]  ->  [library]


Remarque :

The diagram is saved in image format (png), but also includes vector data allowing you to reopen your project later to continue it. You can also save the project in an application-specific format (.excalidraw) which opens directly in Excalidraw when you want to resume your work.
