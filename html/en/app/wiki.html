Library

The PhYBoX integrates the application : Kiwix

It allows you to browse collaborative editing sites such as Wikipedia offline, based on the wiki structure. No need for an internet connection, the data is stored directly on the computer in a single file (a Zim archive).

Folders are already provided to accommodate the most common archives, but to make downloading the PhYBoX easier, they are not included. It is therefore up to you to download this content and put it in the corresponding folder, or create a new one.

Download the archives that you wish to make available to the students and register in the file :

[phybox]  ->  [web]  ->  [app]  ->  [library]  ->  [ma librairie]

When the application starts, Zim archives are automatically recognized and added to the list of applications, in the category where you put them. But not necessarily in the Library, like PHET which is in the Science category.

Download content on KiWiX : library.kiwix.org

For larger sites such as Wikipedia, there may be several versions offered which are limited to a given theme (History, Mathematics, Sciences etc.) or which do not include images and videos. It's up to you to choose the version that will be sufficient for you, depending on the space available on the PC disk.

Examples of Content for KiWiX :

Wikipédia, The free encyclopedia

Vikidia, The encyclopedia for 8 to 13-year-olds

Wiktionary, The free dictionary

Gutenberg, Library of free eBooks

PHET, Interactive Simulations for Science and Math


Customize :

In the archive folder there is an image that serves as an illustration for the application gallery, which you can replace with another, in order to adapt it to your situation.
