Quiz


The PhYBoX integrates the application : Quiz

Developed for PhYBoX, this application allows you to check that students have acquired the different elements of a sequence in a fun way: success is guaranteed !


A Quiz can be played individually so that the student can self-assess or with the whole class. In this case, on an Admin account, you must click on the "game" button to launch a server to which the student tablets connect to play simultaneously.


In individual mode, the scores of each student are recorded and can be viewed by clicking on the "stat" button. In collective mode, at the end of the Quiz, statistics are recorded in a spreadsheet, which can be consulted in the [Classe] -> [Admin] folder or by clicking on the image with the statistics at the top right.

★ Bonus : Doubles the score of a question (win or lose), but it can only be used once.
Ghost Mode : The name is not displayed in Scores, for students who want to play discreetly or strategically.

Note :

Students can also create their own quiz to be played by the all class. They are then saved in the class's [Quiz] folder, but they cannot be played until the teacher has validated them.



Play Mode :      To play in individual or collective mode.






Creation Mode :      To create a new Quiz or edit it.
