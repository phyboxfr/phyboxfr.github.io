MOLECULES


The PHYBoX integrates the application : JsMol

This application was developed to visualize molecules in 3D in the different usual presentations (compact, exploded, stick etc.). The geometry of molecules determines part of their chemical properties, it is important to be able to discover them as completely as possible..

Jsmol can also display molecular models in stereoscopy, so we can see and manipulate molecules in 3D with a pair of anaglyph glasses (red, cyan) which is inexpensive.
