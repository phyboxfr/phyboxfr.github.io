Presentation of this application on the blog : Tice-Éducation.fr


What needs does it meet ? :

Your establishment has tablets and you are looking for a simple way to transmit documents from the computer in your room to the students' tablets ? Conversely, you want to be able to recover on your computer the productions done by the students on their tablet, without any material manipulation.

La PhYBoX was mainly designed with this intention, also offering some applications to help with the production of usual school documents (text, drawing, diagram, audio, photo, quiz). This without having to install anything on the tablets.

It was initially developed for the teaching of Physics, integrating specific applications, but its main functionalities could undoubtedly be useful in all disciplines.

It includes management of class lists with password generation, as well as a system of shortened links to be placed on the digital version of the activity sheet, in order to give direct access to the documents prepared by the teacher.

La PhYBoX also offers tools to simplify the use of synthesis and voice recognition, in order to help students who have difficulty with writing. Finally, it facilitates differentiation, by allowing the teacher to offer several versions of their documents in the same environment, with students being able to choose those that are most suitable for them.

Connected to the class local network, students only have access to documents selected by the teacher. And if the proposed sequence also requires the consultation of websites, it allows you to only authorize the sites chosen by the teacher, blocking all others.

This helps provide peace of mind as the activity unfolds, knowing that students' concentration cannot be distracted by other types of resources they might encounter if they were browsing freely.


Where to start ? :

The PhYBoX website offers tutorials and a Forum to help each other with the implementation and use of this application. You can also offer translations of its interface into languages ​​other than those initially available (German, English, Spanish, French).
