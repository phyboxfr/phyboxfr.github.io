News


This application allows you to offer short videos taken from news or on a given theme at the start of the sequence, in order to contextualize the activity to follow or make the link with current events in the area studied :

Videos should be placed in the [news] folder :

[phybox]  ->  [doc]  ->  [news]

Customize :

You can replace the image which serves as an illustration in the application gallery to adapt it to your use :

[phybox]  ->  [web]  ->  [app]  ->  [news]  ->  news.png
