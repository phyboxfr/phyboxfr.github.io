On the teacher's computer, go to the PhYBoX Control Panel, by clicking on the small wheel of the identification panel on the right :

Open Control Panel






Settings : configures different settings (language, password, etc.)
Applications : selects applications that are available to students
Internet : activates Internet protection, authorizing only the indicated sites
